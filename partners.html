CHURCH-TO-CHURCH • VOLUNTARY NETWORK

Stronger together.

Independent churches. Shared compassion.

We are exploring an interchurch network for worship, community referrals, volunteer cooperation, and responsible local preparedness during storms, power outages, displacement or other emergencies.

We respect each congregation's theology, governance and independence. Joining a conversation is not an agreement to affiliate, fund, transfer authority, or provide services.

Worship. Mutual aid. Preparedness.

A proposed partnership model

Start by listening. Build with trust.

Connect leaders

Introduce pastors and outreach coordinators, identify local needs and share trusted referrals.

Prepare together

Explore communication trees, weather/emergency readiness, and voluntary cooperation without promising a shelter or disaster operation.

Serve responsibly

Consider future food, housing-navigation and family-support projects after funding, safeguarding, insurance and governance are in place.

Where we are: This network is proposed and being organized. No church is described as a participating partner without its explicit agreement. No donations or operational commitments are requested.
