Our mission

Serving people. Strengthening communities.

Thy Kingdom Come Humanity is an emerging faith-centered initiative with a vision to bring congregations, volunteers, and community partners together to serve people facing hardship.

Our long-term aspirations include practical community outreach, charitable housing assistance, and a collaborative network of churches. We are in the foundational planning stage and invite thoughtful partners to help shape the work responsibly.

What guides us

A future initiative

Charitable housing with dignity at its center.

Our housing vision is to help individuals and families facing hardship find safer, more stable footing through partnerships and carefully designed assistance programs.

Housing services are not yet operating. We are assessing legal structure, funding, community needs, and suitable partnerships before making service commitments.
