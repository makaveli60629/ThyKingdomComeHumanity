THE HEART OF OUR MISSION

Everyone has a story.
Everyone deserves compassion.

We believe faith is lived through service: meeting people where they are, listening first, and helping them find a path toward dignity, stability, connection, and hope.

⌂

Hope beyond homelessness

Our vision includes practical outreach and partnerships that help people experiencing housing insecurity reconnect with safe resources and a pathway forward.

✧

Recovery without judgment

People struggling with addiction deserve dignity. We hope to connect individuals with qualified recovery services, supportive communities, and encouragement.

♡

Compassion in every crisis

From grief and illness to financial hardship and family disruption, our aim is to stand alongside people and help connect them with appropriate support.

✿

Food, essentials & care

Future volunteer efforts may support food access, clothing, basic necessities, and neighbor-to-neighbor outreach as resources and partnerships grow.

♡

Faith, fellowship & belonging

Rooted in Christian faith, we welcome people of every religion, spiritual tradition, and those of no faith. You do not have to share our beliefs to find friendship, compassion, and a place to serve the greater good.

✝ ☪ ✡ ॐ ☸ ☬ ☯

Different beliefs. Shared humanity. Everyone belongs.

✦

A fresh start together

Our long-term goal is to help create pathways toward stability through trusted housing, workforce, community, and social-service partnerships.

We are in the planning and formation stage. These are goals, not a claim that shelter, treatment, or direct assistance programs are currently operating.

THE PROMISE THAT GUIDES OUR VISION

THE BIGGER PICTURE

Not just a church.
A movement of humanity.

We envision a network of people and institutions working together so that faith and service can reach far beyond a single building.

Our long-term ambition includes a community church, a separately governed service foundation, responsible housing-support partnerships, and ultimately a wider collaborative network of churches.

OUR NORTH STAR
144churches envisioned

A long-term aspiration—not a claim that 144 churches have joined. We begin with one carefully built foundation.

FROM VISION TO SERVICE

Build with purpose.
Grow with integrity.

Our focus is to create a responsible organization before we promise services or expand into programs.

01
WHERE WE ARE

Build the founding team

Recruit board directors and capable administrative leaders, establish governance and policies, confirm legal structure, and create transparent financial controls.

02
OUR NEXT STEPS

Find partners & launch meaningful outreach

Work with established churches, shelters, recovery providers, community groups, and volunteers; plan carefully scoped events and pilot activities when ready.

03
LONG-TERM VISION

Build pathways to lasting stability

Explore charitable housing assistance, workforce connections, food-growing projects, and a future community campus subject to funding, professional partners, and approvals.

✦

Our longer-term blueprint: Thy Kingdom Come Community Church, a separately organized Kingdom Community Restoration Foundation, and the future Keeper's House support concept. Each would require its own governance, funding, and approvals.

TWO COUNCILS · ONE SHARED MISSION

Shepherds & Divine Warriors

Our proposed Council of Shepherds will guide spiritual teaching and care. Our Divine Warriors will bring faith into action through peaceful outreach, compassionate service, and responsible volunteer support.

Members will be able to pursue service ranks based on preparation, humility, and accountability—not status or spiritual superiority. The councils and ranks are being developed as part of our founding plan.
