FOUNDING-STAGE DATA PRACTICES

Privacy and contact.

We collect only what we need to reply to your inquiry.

This page describes our current intended practices. Thy Kingdom Come Humanity Ministries, Inc. is a proposed, not yet incorporated entity; policies will be reviewed and formally adopted by its governing board.

What we collect

Name, email address, general location, stated interest, organization and role when applicable, and any voluntary message you provide. Please do not send sensitive medical, financial or identification details.

Why we collect it

To reply, determine whether a relevant founding-stage discussion or referral is appropriate, and—only if you separately opt in—to send occasional community-building updates. Submitting a form does not establish church membership, a board position or a paid role.

How form delivery works

The website is hosted as static files on GitHub Pages. Website form submissions are routed through a third-party service, FormSubmit, to the ministry email at thykingdomcomeproject@gmail.com. This service and email provider may process or retain submission information under their own terms. Successful delivery must be verified before this should be treated as a dependable registration system.

Who can access inquiry information

Only people authorized to review founding correspondence should be given access. We do not intend to publish identifiable submissions, post them in GitHub, or sell personal details. We will review access permissions and retention policies as governance is established.

Choice and correction

General updates are optional. You may ask to correct information, stop contact or request deletion from records under our control by emailing thykingdomcomeproject@gmail.com. We will evaluate requests consistent with legal recordkeeping requirements.

Important limitations

No online form is entirely risk-free. Do not submit confidential safeguarding reports or urgent medical, legal, housing or safety requests through these interest forms. The ministry does not yet operate emergency shelters or crisis intervention services. In an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services.

Last updated: November 21, 2026. The ministry does not currently accept charitable contributions through these forms, and no EIN or tax-exempt status is represented here.
