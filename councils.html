ONE FAITH · TWO PATHS OF SERVICE

Shepherds & Divine Warriors.

When we call ourselves part of God's army, we mean a spiritual commitment to prayer, courage, love, and service—not fighting people. As Ephesians 6:12 reminds us, our struggle is not against flesh and blood. We serve our neighbors peacefully and lawfully.

Currently in formation: These councils and service ranks are a proposed structure. Leaders have not yet been appointed, ranks have not yet been conferred, and ministry programs are not represented as operational.

Meet the councilsHow ranks work

TWO COUNCILS, ONE MISSION

Guidance and action.

Every person has equal dignity. The councils will organize distinct responsibilities while sharing a commitment to humility, compassion, and accountability.

SPIRITUAL CARE

Council of Shepherds

Shepherds help guide the community in Scripture, worship, prayer, and pastoral care.

  • Teaching and spiritual mentorship
  • Prayer, listening, and compassionate referrals
  • Fellowship and reconciliation
  • Ethical example and pastoral accountability

Joining: Pastors, ministers, and mature Christians may express interest. Formal teaching or pastoral roles require appropriate review; interest alone does not confer ordination.

FAITH IN ACTION

Divine Warriors

Divine Warriors turn faith into peaceful community service and responsible outreach.

  • Food, essentials, and neighbor-support projects
  • Volunteer outreach and event logistics
  • Connecting people with approved resources
  • Non-confrontational hospitality and event safety support

Joining: Members committed to serving with care and respect may express interest. This is not a police, military, armed, or vigilante group.

OUR PROPOSED SERVICE RANKS

We rise by serving.

Church members may progress through an accountable rank pathway. A rank means defined responsibilities earned through preparation, reliable service, and trust—not higher spiritual worth, special access to God, or power over other people.

  1. RANK 01Fellowship MemberParticipates in worship, fellowship, and community service.
  2. RANK 02ApprenticeChooses a service track and learns the ministry's standards.
  3. RANK 03Commissioned ServantCompletes applicable orientation and screening, then serves in an approved role.
  4. RANK 04StewardCoordinates volunteers or projects with accountability.
  5. RANK 05Council LeadOversees a ministry team after review and formal appointment.

The Shepherds and Divine Warriors may later use track-specific titles, subject to the founding charter. These names and promotion criteria are proposals, not an announcement of existing rank holders. Donations, recruitment numbers, or membership fees will not buy a rank.

A covenant of humility and safety

Service comes before titles. Volunteers will be subject to a written code of conduct, role-appropriate screening and training, transparent appointment decisions, and processes to report concerns or remove leaders for misconduct.

No violence, intimidation, weapons-based activity, threats, vigilantism, forced participation, coercive control, or discrimination is authorized. Spiritual leadership will not substitute for licensed professional care or lawful public safety services.

FROM VISION TO SERVICE

Build properly.
Then grow.

1 · Establish governance

Approve a founding charter, code of conduct, safeguarding policies, transparent financial controls, and leadership standards.

2 · Invite candidates

Collect interest from Shepherds, Divine Warriors, administrators, and volunteers. Interview and screen before assignments.

3 · Pilot one service project

Work with community partners, record lessons, and expand only when appropriate leadership and resources are in place.
