About the editions

The Ethiopian Orthodox study collection contains historical English translations of Enoch and Jubilees, not a verified complete Ethiopian Orthodox Bible. The King James Version with Apocrypha is a separate edition. The classical works are provided for historical study, not as statements of ministry doctrine.

Chapter sources: eBible.org, Une Sacra (Enoch), Christian Classics Ethereal Library (Jubilees), Wikisource (Sun Tzu), and Toronto Metropolitan University Libraries (Machiavelli). Full PDF files remain hosted by their respective providers.
