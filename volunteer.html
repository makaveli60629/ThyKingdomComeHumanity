VOLUNTEER WITH PURPOSE

Come as you are. Help hope grow.

We are building the foundation before launching programs.

No special experience is necessary to express interest. If you have the time, a skill to share, local knowledge, or simply the desire to help people, there may be a place for you as this ministry takes shape.

Early planning stage · Opportunities will be defined as leadership and resources are established

Everyone belongs. Every act of compassion counts.

Ways to get involved

Help build from the beginning.

We are seeking people who can help shape a safe, welcoming, transparent organization, not advertising established service programs or current staff vacancies.

Community connections

Introduce the mission to potential partners, local organizations, and people who understand community needs.

Events and outreach planning

Help explore future activities, coordinate volunteers, and design services with appropriate safeguards.

Administrative support

Contribute organization, communications, scheduling, records, and project planning skills.

Location and launch insight

Suggest suitable states or communities outside Chicago, including practical information about local partners and opportunities.

Volunteering is exploratory and unpaid at this stage. Any role or activity depends on an approved plan, appropriate screening where needed, and resources. No direct shelter, treatment, or assistance is presently promised.

Responsible growth

Our vision needs guardians.

The co-founder intends to help launch this ministry and transfer routine operations to a qualified, accountable team—not to place the organization under unchecked control. Before programs begin, we intend to establish governing documents, independent board oversight, financial controls, and clear volunteer responsibilities.

The state where the first church will be established remains undecided. We welcome thoughtful ideas from those with ties to communities outside Chicago.
