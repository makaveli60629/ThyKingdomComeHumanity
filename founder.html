ABOUT THE CO-FOUNDER

Building systems. Connecting people. Serving a greater purpose.

Ronald brings a diverse, hands-on professional background to the founding of Thy Kingdom Come Humanity Ministry. His experience includes serving as Chairman of Sojoy USA, alongside engineering and technology, digital product development, marketing strategy, restaurant ownership and hospitality, and penetration testing. He values practical problem-solving, respect for people of every background, and communities that can sustain themselves through trustworthy leadership.

A LEGACY OF STEWARDSHIP

The foundation is our responsibility. Its future belongs to the community.

My task is to help create a place where people come as they are, where spiritual guidance meets practical care, and where capable leaders are empowered to serve. We are not trying to build a monument to one person. We are working to build something that can continue beyond any one founder — through the faith, commitment, compassion and accountability of its members.
