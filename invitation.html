Help build a church for humanity.

Not a church built around one person. A mission built to outlast any one person.

Thy Kingdom Come Community Church is taking shape around a simple belief: people facing hard seasons deserve compassion, dignity, community, and a chance to begin again. We are inviting people of every background to help establish its foundation with care and integrity.

Faith · Compassion · Renewal

A message from the co-founder

Born from the journey. Built for others.

Life's struggles, loss, and seasons of rebuilding helped shape my conviction that no one should have to face their hardest chapter without compassion. The personal journey I've shared through TikTok is part of the inspiration behind this mission. The ministry's purpose, however, is much bigger than my story.

“I see my calling as helping build the foundation, bringing the right people together, and then returning to the work ahead. The church should be guided by accountable leaders and protected by a structure that serves people, not personal interests.”

This is a vision for a lasting institution with clear responsibilities, independent oversight, financial safeguards, and a thoughtful transfer of day-to-day leadership. The co-founder's future writing and film projects are separate from the ministry's charitable work.

— R. Chadee, Co-Founder

Where we are now

A vision becoming a foundation.

01 · TODAY

Build the founding team

Invite prospective board directors, administrators, volunteers, and advisers who believe in ethical service and careful governance.

02 · NEXT

Establish the right structure

Choose a state, seek legal and tax guidance, adopt governing documents and safeguards, and determine an appropriate 501(c)(3) pathway.

03 · OVER TIME

Serve and grow responsibly

Build partnerships and fund pilot outreach before exploring future housing, recovery-support referrals, and a wider network of churches.

We are organizing, not claiming that a 501(c)(3) determination has been issued or that the programs described are currently operating. Churches may qualify for federal tax exemption under specific rules; our structure and any formal recognition have not been finalized.

Where should we begin?

We have not chosen a state in which to establish the first church. We are exploring a location outside the city of Chicago, and considering community connections, practical needs, and possible opportunities in places such as Oregon. No destination or partner has been confirmed.

If you know a community that could benefit, understand a state's nonprofit requirements, or have contacts who could help build a responsible launch, please tell us. Your perspective can help shape where this ministry begins.
